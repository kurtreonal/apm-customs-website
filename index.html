About APM Customs Brokerage

APM Customs Brokerage was established last February 24, 2011 for the sole purpose of handling customs clearance formalities of one company who requested to have a broker to handle fully all their company importation and exportation. For the first year the company is exclusively attending to this client. As we continuously provide excellent service to our client, from one company we were able to build network of list of companies thru referral from satisfied clients.

APM Customs Brokerage remain committed to deliver the best service to our clients from Customs Clearance, logistics, consultation until delivery. We may not be a big player in this industry but our service extended to our customers is beyond their expectation.

How It Works

Our seamless step-by-step customs clearance methodology ensures your cargo is filed and cleared on schedule.

01
Inquiry

03

04
